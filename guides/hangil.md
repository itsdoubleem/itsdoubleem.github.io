---
app: hangil
title: How to use HANGIL
lead: There is almost nothing to set up. Open it, do what the top card says, and come back to Review each day.
outcome: HANGIL on your own phone, set to your trade if you have one, with a Korean voice you can hear. It is free, it works with no connection after the first load, and there is no account at any point.
before:
  heading: Before you start
  body: You need the app and a phone that can speak Korean. Nothing else, and nothing to fill in.
  items:
    - The app. Download it for Android from the HANGIL page, or open it in your browser and add it to your home screen — on Android, Chrome's menu › Add to Home screen. Android warns you about installing a file from outside the store. That is normal for an app shared this way, and you can allow it for this one file.
    - A Korean voice on your phone. If yours does not have one, the app says so on its first screen rather than staying silent.
    - Nothing else. No account, no email, and no signal once it has loaded.
steps:
  - image: 01-today
    alt: The Today screen, headed 어서 오세요 — welcome. A large card at the top reads Next, the alphabet, lesson 1 of 8, The six plain vowels. Below it are four tiles — The alphabet, 0 of 8 lessons; Course, 0 of 24 units; Vocabulary, 197 words; and EPS-TOPIK exam practice.
    kicker: Step 1
    title: Open it, and do what the top card says
    body: The first screen is Today. The big card at the top always names the next thing to do. The first time you open it, that is lesson 1 of the alphabet. Tap it and start.
    note: Every screen after this one is written in 한글, the Korean alphabet, so the alphabet comes first. If you can already read 한글, go straight to Course — the app says to skip the alphabet lessons if you can already read it.
  - image: 02-trade
    alt: Settings, with Trade — 업종 set to 식품가공 — Food processing, and the explanation beneath it that the job-related questions on your paper come from the group you chose on your application.
    kicker: Step 2
    title: If you are applying for factory work, set your trade
    body: In Settings, under Trade — 업종, choose the job group you picked on your application. There are eight, from 고무·플라스틱 rubber and plastic to 펄프·종이·목재 pulp, paper and wood. The practice papers then put eight questions from your trade in the reading half, as the real paper does.
    note: For agriculture, fishing, construction or service work, leave it on Not chosen. Those papers use common questions only, and the app does not pretend otherwise.
  - image: 03-voice
    alt: Settings, showing Korean voice set to Automatic — Yuna, Speech speed at 1.00×, and a Test the voice button, with the explanation that slowing a voice down smears the consonants.
    kicker: Step 3
    title: Check that you can hear the Korean
    body: Tap Test the voice. If you hear a Korean sentence, you are set. Every Korean sentence in the app has a play button, and it is your own phone that speaks it, so it works with no signal.
    note: The voice name will be different on your phone — this picture was taken in a browser on a computer. On the Android app, when your phone has more than one speech engine or Korean voice, Settings lets you choose. They do not all sound alike, so it is worth a minute to find one you can listen to. Leave the speed at 1.00.
after:
  - image: 04-learn
    alt: A lesson card headed What changes, 2 of 14, in the unit Saying what something is. It shows 학생 above 학생이에요, each with a play button and a Show meaning button, and a note that nothing was added but the ending, and a bare noun became a whole sentence.
    kicker: A lesson
    title: One card at a time, the Korean first
    body: Each unit walks through its grammar one card at a time. Many cards show the same sentence twice with one thing different, like 학생 and 학생이에요, so you can see exactly what changed. Tap the play button to hear it.
    note: The English stays hidden until you tap Show meaning. Try to read the Korean first — that is the part you are here to learn.
  - image: 05-practice
    alt: A listening question, What did you hear?, with a Play button. Answer A, It's my phone, is marked red; answer B, It's my passport, is marked green; below them the app says Not that one, above a Next button.
    kicker: Practice
    title: Then practise it
    body: After the cards, the unit gives you questions — choose the meaning, put the words in order, or listen and pick what you heard. Tap Play to hear a listening question.
    note: Get one wrong and it shows you the right answer straight away. It also comes back in Review, starting again from today.
  - image: 06-review
    alt: The Review screen on question 1 of 3, from the vocabulary set Money and wages, asking for the Korean for monthly wage, with four answers — 영수증, 급여명세서, 월급 and 현금. The Review tab at the bottom carries a badge reading 3.
    kicker: Every day
    title: Come back to Review
    body: Everything you answer in the alphabet, the course and the vocabulary comes back in Review at a widening gap. When something is due, the Review tab at the bottom shows how many. Clearing them is the few minutes a day that makes the rest stick.
    note: The numbers in this picture come from sample progress made for the screenshot, not from anyone's study. Exam questions you miss are kept separately, on the EPS-TOPIK screen.
  - image: 07-weak
    alt: The Weak spots screen, saying 2 topics are costing you more than the rest, ordered worst first. The first is 에 against 에서, 3 of 12 right, 25%, with a Practise 6 of these button; the second is 있다 and 없다, 6 of 8 right.
    kicker: When you have answered a few
    title: See what you keep getting wrong
    body: Weak spots lists the topics that cost you the most, worst first — not just how many you got wrong, but what they were about, like 에 against 에서. Tap Practise to get questions on that one thing, pulled from everywhere it turns up.
    note: A topic appears only once it has six answers behind it. Fewer than that is a small sample, not a weak spot. The scores here are sample progress, as above.
  - image: 08-exam
    alt: The EPS-TOPIK screen, explaining that the paper has forty questions, twenty listening and twenty reading, in fifty minutes. Below are How the exam works, six short reads, and a list headed By question type, starting with Signs and notices and The right word.
    kicker: The exam
    title: Learn each kind of question
    body: The Exam tab is practice for the paper itself. Start with How the exam works, then go through the question types one at a time — signs and notices, the right word, and the rest — so nothing on the day is a surprise.
  - image: 09-paper
    alt: Practice paper 1, 모의고사 1회 — forty questions in 50 minutes, nothing marked until you hand it in, and the clock does not stop — with a Start the paper button at the foot.
    kicker: When you are ready
    title: Sit a timed paper
    body: There are two practice papers, forty questions each, fifty minutes, with a clock that does not stop. Nothing is marked until you hand it in. Then you go through what you got wrong, with the reason, one question at a time.
    note: The questions were written for this app in the shape of the real paper. They are not a past paper, and the app cannot tell you whether you will pass — it scores you out of 200 and stops there.
  - image: 10-backup
    alt: The Your progress section of Settings, saying everything the app knows about you is on this device and that clearing the browser's data deletes your progress, with Save a backup, Restore a backup and Erase everything buttons.
    kicker: Now and then
    title: Save a backup
    body: Your progress lives only on your phone. There is no account to get it back from. In Settings, under Your progress, tap Save a backup and keep the file somewhere safe. Restore a backup brings it back on a new phone or a new browser.
    note: In a browser, clearing the browser's data deletes your progress. Take a backup before you change phone or browser.
